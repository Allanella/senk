About SENK

St. Elizabeth Senior Secondary School Nkoowe has been a beacon of educational excellence and Catholic values since 1995.

Our Journey

Our Journey

“

Founded in 1995, St. Elizabeth Senior Secondary School Nkoowe was established with a vision to provide quality, affordable Catholic education that transforms young minds. What began as a modest institution has grown into a beacon of educational excellence across Uganda.

Over nearly three decades, SENK has remained committed to its core mission: fostering academic excellence, spiritual growth, moral development, and disciplined character in every student. Our Catholic heritage remains central to all we do, guided by the principles of faith, hope, and charity.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding SENK's commitment to excellence and Catholic values

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence providing holistic education that shapes spiritually mature, academically excellent, disciplined, and innovative leaders who impact society positively.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable Catholic education that develops students intellectually, morally, physically, and spiritually, preparing them for success in higher education and meaningful contribution to society.
