Admission Requirements

Certificate of PLE/UCE results or a report card from your former school.

Personal Items to Bring

  • Hand watch
  • School shoes, open shoes
  • Pens and pencils
  • Bucket and jerrycan

  • Washing soap
  • Beddings: 2 pairs of bedsheets, a blanket, and a mosquito net
